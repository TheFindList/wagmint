Travel Guide

A saveable pet-travel planning checklist

Good pet travel starts before the car moves. Different species tolerate travel very differently, so the safest plan is one built around secure transport, familiar routines and realistic limits.

Before the trip

Confirm identification, destination rules, lodging policies and any required health documents. Locate veterinary care near your destination and save the information where it is available offline.

Secure transport matters most

Use a carrier, restraint or travel setup appropriate for the animal and vehicle. Avoid allowing pets to move freely around a moving vehicle, and never place an animal where airbag deployment could create added risk.

Pack familiar essentials

Bring the animal's normal food, water plan, medication, cleanup supplies, bedding or familiar comfort item, identification records and any habitat equipment needed at the destination.

Manage temperature and stops

Plan breaks based on the animal's needs, and never leave a pet unattended in a vehicle where temperatures can become dangerous. Birds, reptiles, aquatics and small mammals may need specialized temperature or habitat planning.

Have a backup plan

Know what you will do if lodging changes, a carrier fails, medication is lost or your pet becomes ill. A small amount of advance planning can prevent a minor problem from becoming a trip-ending emergency.

Safety note: This checklist is general information. Ask a qualified veterinarian for advice about travel suitability, anxiety, medication, vaccination or species-specific transport needs.
