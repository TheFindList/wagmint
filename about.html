About Wagmint

Every kind of pet deserves thoughtful care.

Wagmint is a pet-lifestyle discovery site for dogs, cats, birds, small animals, reptiles, aquatics and more. We organize useful finds, practical ideas and save-worthy guides so pet people can spend less time scrolling and more time caring for the animals they love.

Safety comes first. Pet needs vary widely, so recommendations should always be checked for the specific species, size, age, health and behavior of your animal. Follow product instructions and seek qualified veterinary or species-specific guidance when appropriate.
